# GATE-6 — Campus Rush

Open `gate6-campus-rush.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox (desktop or phone).
Sound starts on your first tap or key press (browser rule).

## What's in this folder
- `gate6-campus-rush.html` — the whole game. The 23 classmates' voice recordings, the Kill 1–3 hit
  sounds, the Director's-room and Caught cues and the campus background track are all embedded in it,
  so they work even when you double-click the file.
- `assets/voices/` — those same recordings, converted from AAC/MP4 to MP3 (mono, 64 kbps, trimmed,
  loudness-matched). Kept here as the source files; the game doesn't need them.
- `assets/music/` — the four party songs (your files, renamed to what the game looks for).
  `background-the-builder.mp3` is the campus background track (source copy; the game has it built in).
- `assets/sfx/` — source copies of Kill 1–3, the Director's-room cue and the Caught cue (built in).
- `assets/adhiraj/` — optional recordings (see the README inside).

## Sounds and where they play
- **Kill 1 / 2 / 3** — one at random each time someone is knocked down (never the same one twice in
  a row, slight pitch variation, at most two at once).
- **Campus background** — loops the whole time you're on campus; pauses inside The Terrace.
  Follows the Music slider.
- **Director's room** — plays as you walk into Adhiraj's office; he starts talking about 1.6 s later
  (click to skip ahead). Fades when you leave.
- **Caught** — plays on the "busted" card when police or security catch you; fades when you continue.
- **The Terrace from outside** — the DJ set leaks out as you get close, muffled at a distance and
  clearer at the door.

## Days, saves and money (new)
- **The day doesn't end the game.** At 23:00 you get a Day Summary (missions, money earned and spent,
  knockdowns, best combo, time taken, reputation, prize money), then **Start Day 2**. Money, unlocks,
  character, outfits, reputation and the knockdown scoreboard all carry over.
- **Each new day** serves 5 missions (below) with tighter timers, one more guard on patrol (up to six
  extra), faster guards, and stars that take longer to fade.
- **Characters:** see below. Your character is saved with the game.
- **Saving:** automatic when you enter any building, finish a mission, start a new day, use the PG,
  or press Save game in the pause menu. The start screen shows **Continue** with the day, time and money.
  If you're busted or collapse, you come back at your last checkpoint. If the browser blocks storage,
  the game plays normally, just without saves.
- **Money:**
  - Earn: missions, side quests, DJ tips, selling things you find on the ground (they sparkle),
    hackathon prize money, Vlogger reels.
  - Spend in the **store** (B, the cart button, or tap the wallet): Yulu pass ₹500, scooter ₹3,000,
    car ₹12,000, the Director's SUV ₹40,000, plus upgrades and outfits.
  - Also on campus: food at Breakfast Lounge and Cafeteria, chai, Guru Bar drinks, an auto between
    Gate-6 and Gate-7 (₹100), and bribing a guard (₹800 a star, only at 1–2 stars).
- **Rent:** your PG on the main road (see below), ₹300–700 a night. Unpaid rent comes out of your locker if the wallet is short.
  - After 18:00 you can sleep there: the day ends early and you wake with full energy.
  - Crash anywhere else: 70 energy the next day.
  - Can't pay the rent: no bed, so you start the next day on 40 energy.
  - The wallet flashes red from 20:00 until the rent is paid.

## Learning the game (new)
- **Tutorial:** the first time you play, a 90-second walkthrough: move, slap, take a bike, enter a building, read the
  objective. The clock waits while you do it. Skip it any time; it never comes back.
- **Press ?** (or the **?** button) for the controls card, any time.
- **Colours mean the same thing everywhere:** **gold** = main mission, **cyan** = side activity,
  **red** = pursuit or danger, **green** = money and rewards.
- **Objective panel:** always says **what** to do, **where** (place + distance) and **how long** (time left, par, or
  when the day ends).
- **Waypoint:** a big gold arrow with the distance on it (a cyan one for a side quest). The minimap has a legend
  and a compass (N turns when the camera turns).
- **Floating prompts:** anything you can use shows its key above it when you're in range (E, Space, H).
- **Day summary** ends with what to try next.

## Speedrun (new)
- Every mission and every full day (all of a day's missions) is timed. Your best times are kept in this browser.
- **Leaderboard:** press **L** or the trophy button. Each mission shows your best, the campus record and your rank;
  click a row for the full board. Also: best full day and your **Perfect Day** count. **Tab** still opens the
  knockdown scoreboard.
- Eight **campus rivals** (fictional, marked as such) are on every board so it's never empty.
- **Ghost:** a translucent copy of your best run plays alongside you at the same moment, in missions and across
  the whole day. When it says *BEST FINISHED*, you're behind.
- The mission card and the end-of-day card show your time against your best, your rank and anything you beat.

## The main road (new)
The road outside the gates is now a strip of Bengaluru. The campus has a wall: **Gate-6 and Gate-7 are the only ways out**.

**Your PG.** Four PGs line the far side of the road. Pick one on Day 1 (walk in and choose *Make this my PG*);
if you never do, SS PG takes you in at 23:00. You can move later for ₹500.

| PG | Rent | Perk |
|---|---|---|
| SS PG | ₹400 | Right across from Gate-6 |
| Monk Mansion | ₹300 | Cheapest; wake up with +5% build |
| Countryside Raindance | ₹550 | Free breakfast thali every day (+30 energy) |
| Uniworld | ₹700 | Locker money earns 3% a night |

At home: sleep (after 18:00: saves, full energy, next day), pay rent, change character, and a **locker** for cash
(money in the locker is safe from traffic-police fines). The other three PGs have residents to talk to and a side
quest each: a **tiffin run** to a campus building, a **puff run** to the Iyengar Bakery, or a race against
**Team Overfit**, the rival team (they live at Countryside Raindance, or Uniworld if that's your home).

**On the road:** a tea stall opposite Gate-6, a darshini, an Iyengar bakery, a dosa cart, a Yulu dock (bikes on the
footpath) and the metro line overhead. Two dead-end lanes off the footpath are good places to hide.

**Traffic.** Four lanes, keep left. Autos weave, cut in and stop without warning; buses pull in and block the kerb
lane while they load; bikes filter through the gaps. Anything stuck honks; total honking is capped so it stays
funny. Nobody honks at the cow.
- **Jams** lock a stretch (by Monk Mansion or by Countryside Raindance) for 20–40 s, more often at rush hour
  (08:30–10:30, 17:30–20:30). Bollards stop cars from using the footpath. **Bikes fit through; cars don't.**
- **Potholes and speed breakers** jolt you and cost speed. Slow down for them.
- **Rain** slows traffic, sends everyone on the footpath under the awnings, and turns the road into a mirror.

**Police.** Campus guards don't leave campus: getting out of a gate is a real escape. But at **3+ stars** security
calls the **traffic police**, who patrol the main road on bikes. If they catch you, the fine comes out of your wallet.

## Missions (new)
Twelve mission types; each day serves five. Walk to a mission marker (the map shows them) and press **E**
for the briefing card. During a mission the clock runs at quarter speed, so a 4-minute mission costs
about one in-game hour. Each mission has a live objective, a timer and a star rating: **3★ means fast
and clean** (no alarms, no busts, nothing broken). Pay scales with stars and with the day number. A
failed mission stays on the day's list; try again any time before 23:00.

| Tier | Missions | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chai Run · Yulu Time Trial · Crowd Control · Recovery · The Pitch | from Day 1 (this is the fixed Day 1 set) |
| 2 | Defuse · The Impostor · Two-Person Job | 3★ on any two Tier-1 missions |
| 3 | Heist · The Basement · All-Nighter | 3★ on any two Tier-2 missions |
| Special | Demo Day | only on days 3, 6, 9… |

- **Chai Run:** seven cups, each cooling on its own timer.
- **Yulu Time Trial:** ring road plus main road; a free Yulu is lent if you don't own the pass. Best
  times go on a leaderboard.
- **Crowd Control:** clear the crowd in 90 s. Slapping works but it's loud (heat).
- **Recovery:** find 5 witnesses across campus and the main road, then catch the thief.
- **The Pitch:** rhythm minigame (Space / Enter, or tap); mentors interrupt.
- **Defuse:** 4 minutes to reach the Innovation Lab and solve the wire + command panel; being spotted
  by a guard costs 20 s.
- **The Impostor:** do 5 tasks while someone sabotages (energy drain, lights out, doors locked).
  Sabotage drops clues; collect 3, call the meeting at Main Hall and accuse. **A wrong accusation wipes
  the day's build progress.**
- **Two-Person Job:** a teammate follows you. Press **T** (or the T button on phones) for orders:
  1 Wait · 2 Follow · 3 Distract the nearest guard · 4 Release. A guarded door plus two more puzzles.
- **Heist:** steal the laptop from Tower 2 and reach Gate-7 before 4 stars; the Director's office
  escape room is the bail-out (1★).
- **The Basement:** dark rooms under the Workshop, torch cone only; pressure plates, a lever puzzle,
  8 relics.
- **All-Nighter:** opens at 21:30 and runs to 04:00. Energy drains twice as fast; chai carts act as
  checkpoints.
- **Demo Day:** sprint, chase, then pitch.

**Streaks and bonuses**
- **Daily streak:** finish 3 or more missions each day to keep it; +₹150 per streak day (capped at 10).
- **Perfect Day:** all 5 missions, no busts → bonus of ₹1,000 and rising each day.
- The Day Summary shows missions done, stars out of 15, the streak, and the **next unlock**.

## Party songs (The Terrace)
Your four songs are in `assets/music/`. Walk into The Terrace and one of them starts at random; when it
ends the next one plays (shuffled, no repeats until all four have played).

- **Pick a song:** the &#9776; Playlist button in the party panel, or press **1–4** (keyboard).
  Press **L** to open/close the list. ⏮ / ⏭ still skip back and forward.
- **House loop:** the last row of the playlist (or the House loop button) switches to the game's own
  generated track; pick any song to go back.
- Keep the `assets` folder next to the HTML: the songs are read from there, not built into the HTML.
  Moved the HTML somewhere else? Use **Load songs** (Pause > Audio mixer) once and pick the four files;
  they're then saved in that browser.

## Dancing (The Terrace)
- **SPACE** (or DANCE on phones) to dance, **X** (or NEXT MOVE) to change your move:
  Bhangra, Disco point, Hands up, Lightbulb, Thumka, Airplane, Clap, Shuffle, Fist pump, Sway.
  A new move on the dance floor gets a little extra crowd hype.
- The crowd changes moves every 16 beats, and every so often the whole floor does the same move together.
